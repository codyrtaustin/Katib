# Adding Katib to Your Dock

The Katib app bundle has been created at `~/Katib/Katib.app`.

## Option 1: Add to Applications Folder (Recommended)

1. Open Finder
2. Navigate to your home directory (`~`)
3. Go to the `Katib` folder
4. Drag `Katib.app` to your `Applications` folder (or press Cmd+Shift+A to go to Applications)
5. Once in Applications, you can:
   - Right-click the app and select "Options" > "Keep in Dock"
   - Or drag it directly to your Dock

## Option 2: Add Directly to Dock

1. Open Finder
2. Navigate to `~/Katib/`
3. Drag `Katib.app` directly to your Dock
4. The app will stay in your Dock for easy access

## Option 3: Create Alias/Shortcut

1. Right-click on `Katib.app` in Finder
2. Select "Make Alias"
3. Drag the alias to your Desktop or Dock

## Launching the App

- Double-click `Katib.app` from Finder
- Click the icon in your Dock
- Use Spotlight (Cmd+Space) and type "Katib"
- Use Launchpad

The app will launch the Katib GUI application automatically.
